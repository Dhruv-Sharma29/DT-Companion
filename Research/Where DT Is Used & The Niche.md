# 🗺️ Where DT Is Used, and Where the Niche Is

**8 Sep 2026**

## Where Design Thinking actually gets used

| Context | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Education** — engineering, design, MBA, HCI | Often a *mandatory* course. Huge population. |
| **Corporate innovation labs & training** | IBM, SAP and consultancies run DT at scale internally |
| **Product / UX teams** | Uses the synthesis muscle daily, rarely calls it "DT" |
| **Social innovation & development** | NGOs, public health, community programmes |
| **Public sector / policy labs** | Government service design |
| **Healthcare** | Care pathways, patient experience |
| **Startups, incubators, hackathons** | Compressed timelines, highest premature-convergence risk |

> [!important] Volume vs. practice #key
> Far more people are **taught** Design Thinking than **practise** it professionally.
> The largest population by a wide margin is students and corporate trainees — and that is
> exactly the population that needs **friction, not speed** ([[The Automation Paradox]]).
> The big market and the right market point the same way. That is unusual; use it. #insight

## The niche

Not "Design Thinking tools" — too broad, crowded by Miro and FigJam. The niche is the
**Observe → Define handoff**, the moment nobody owns, for teams who are **learning** the
process rather than performing it.

## Naming the area — "staying in the flow"

You asked what this area is called. Careful: say "flow" in a pitch and people hear
Csikszentmihalyi's flow *state*. You mean something different — staying oriented in a
process. The precise terms: #key

- **Metacognitive scaffolding** — support for planning, monitoring and evaluating your own
  thinking. This is the exact term for *knowing where you are and whether it is going well.*
- **Procedural / process scaffolding** — support for *what to do next*.
- **Scaffolding & fading** (Wood, Bruner & Ross) — support is deliberately **removed** as
  competence grows. That is the mechanism that resolves the automation paradox: friction that
  retires itself. Best single concept in this note. #insight
- **Self-regulated learning** (Zimmerman) — the education-research home for all of it;
  **co-regulation** when it's a team.
- **Reflection-in-action** (Donald Schön) — adjusting while the work is happening.

**Use: "metacognitive scaffolding for design practice."** Precise, defensible, and it puts
the project in a real research tradition instead of an invented category.
