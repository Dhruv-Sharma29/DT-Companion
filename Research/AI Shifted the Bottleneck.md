# ⚙️ AI Shifted the Bottleneck

The core argument behind [[Problem Definition]]. **8 Sep 2026**

## The shape of the claim
When one step in a process becomes very cheap, the constraint does not disappear —
it **moves downstream** to the next step that resisted automation. The work that was
always hard becomes the work that is now *visibly* hard, because nothing else is in
front of it. #key

In Design Thinking, AI made two steps cheap:
- **collecting** (interviews transcribed, notes summarised, desk research instant)
- **generating** (ideas, HMWs, concepts, variations — unlimited)

Both of those are *production*. Neither is *judgement*. So judgement is the new constraint.

## Precedents — this pattern is not new #source
| Domain | What got cheap | Where the constraint went |
|---|---|---|
| Photography | capture (film → digital) | curation and editing |
| Search | access to information | judging credibility |
| Software | writing code | deciding what to build, reviewing it |
| Publishing | distribution | earning attention |

Using a precedent is a strong pitch move: it shows the shift is structural, not a fashion. #insight

## But it is more than a relative shift
Three mechanisms make judgement **absolutely harder**, not just relatively more important:

### 1. Volume
More evidence and more ideas than a team can hold in working memory. Selection cost rises
with the size of the pile, and the pile is now unbounded.

### 2. Provenance(origin) collapse #key
Effort used to be a **grounding signal**. If you wrote the note, you were in the room.
AI severs effort from evidence: a synthesised claim and an observed one look identical on
the page — both fluent, both confident, both undated and unattributed.

> [!warning] The dangerous property is not that AI is wrong.
> It is that AI output is *plausible(that you can believe) without provenance* — well-formed enough that the
> team stops asking where it came from.

### 3. Premature convergence
A well-formed answer arriving early suppresses divergence. Teams anchor on the first
coherent(clear and easy to understand) story because it is coherent, not because it is supported.

### 4. Skipped reps (the student-specific one)
Novices build judgement by doing the synthesis badly and being corrected. If the tool does
that step, the rep never happens. The deficit compounds across a degree. #insight

## Consequence for the product
The scarce resource is no longer information or ideas. It is **justified conviction** —
being able to say *why this reading, and not that one, deserves the next unit of effort*.

That is what the product should be selling. #key
