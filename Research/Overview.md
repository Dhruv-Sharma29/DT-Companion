# 🧭 Overview — Problem and Solution

**14 Sep 2026.** One-page summary of the current position. Detail: [[Core Statement]] ·
[[Team U Deck — Know Everything]] · [[Presentation Prep]].

## The problem
**AI gives every founder the same answer.** Student startup aspirants gather interviews, notes and
AI output across ChatGPT, Notion and Miro, and lose track of what users actually said versus what
they or the AI concluded. So they pick the first problem that sounds right, often one that's
already crowded, and build on assumptions they never tested.

**Why it matters:** 43% of failed startups had poor product-market fit (CB Insights, 2026). In a
startup, the wrong problem is fatal.

**Root cause:** jump to a solution ← research scattered ← no structured process ← rely on AI to
define the problem ← AI returns generic answers, not their own evidence.

## Who has it
- **Primary:** students who intend to build a startup — especially engineers, who are trained to
  solve problems, not choose them.
- **Secondary:** mentors and faculty, who see the pitch but not the reasoning.
- **Buyer:** college incubators and E-Cells.

## Why now
- India is the **3rd-largest** startup ecosystem, with **2 lakh+** recognised startups (Dec 2025).
- **~50%** come from Tier II/III cities, where mentors are scarce.
- **32.5%** of college students are already nascent entrepreneurs (GUESSS 2023).
- India has **~0.8 incubators per million people**, versus 8–10 in the US, UK and China.

## The solution
An **iPad app built on procedural scaffolding**: it guides founders from research to a validated
problem, and it asks questions instead of giving answers.

| It does | So the founder can tell |
|---|---|
| Marks each note: *saw it · secondhand · concluded · assumed* | what they actually know |
| Asks the question they skipped | where their reasoning jumps |
| Requires an alternative before committing | whether they settled too early |
| Shows what already exists, with sources | whether the problem is **taken** |
| Surfaces observations that don't fit | what makes it **theirs** |
| Traces a failed test back to the broken assumption | whether to change the problem, idea or build |
| Runs a Mock Crit before the real one | where they'll be challenged |

**Rule:** AI notices, the founder decides. Support fades as they improve.

**Why iPad:** on-device AI keeps interview data private, works offline, captures speech and
photos, and shows a reasoning chain whole.

## Different from
ChatGPT gives the average answer · Miro and Notion store notes but not reasoning · Dovetail is for
professionals · Design Kit teaches methods but doesn't stay with you.
