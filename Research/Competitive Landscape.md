## The hole #key
- Whiteboards have **no process and no memory**.
- Repositories **assume the expertise** that novices lack.
- Innovation platforms manage **ideas, not evidence**.
- Content kits **teach but don't accompany**.
- AI tools **do the thinking**, which is the problem.

> Nothing occupies: **accompanies you while you work, teaches judgement by withholding
> answers, and remembers why you decided.**

## Positioning line
Not "Miro but smarter." Closer to: *a lab notebook that asks you questions* —
see [[Terminology & Prior Art]] on the reasoning ledger.

## The competitor to take seriously
Not another tool — **ChatGPT plus a Miro board**, free, already open on their screen.
Beating "good enough and already there" is the real bar. Your answer has to be an outcome
they can't get that way: **their reasoning got better**, and someone can see that it did.
