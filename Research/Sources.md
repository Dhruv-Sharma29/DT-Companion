# 🔗 Sources

**Started 10 Sep 2026.** Every external claim, link and citation used in this project.
Anything presented as fact should be traceable to a line here. #key

## How to add one
```
- **[Short label]** — what it supports · [link](url) · read? · added DD Mon YYYY
```
Mark **read?** honestly: `read` / `skimmed` / `unread — cited from abstract`.
An unread source is not evidence, and being caught on one costs more than the citation gained.
