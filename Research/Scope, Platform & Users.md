# 🎯 Scope, Platform, Users — Decisions

**8 Sep 2026**

## Who is it for #key

| Role | Who | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary user** | **Student startup aspirants** — any degree, defined by intent to build. **Changed 11 Sep 2026** → [[User — Student Startup Aspirants]] | *Is it real, is it taken, is it mine?* — answered with evidence |

### The instructor is the strategic key #key
Classic education split: **the user is not the buyer.** But there's more to it here.

Earlier we concluded that students are *graded on the artifact, not the reasoning*, and that
"no tool defeats an incentive" ([[Why Students Struggle — Causal Factors]], factor #9).
A reasoning ledger **changes what can be assessed** — it makes reasoning visible, therefore
gradeable, therefore worth doing.

> [!important] The instructor isn't just the buyer. They're the mechanism by which the
> structural cause gets fixed. That resolves the one factor we'd written off as untouchable. #insight

## Start point and endpoint #key

- **Start** — *"We have material and don't know what it means."*
  Notes, transcripts, photos exist. Nothing has been concluded yet.
- **End** — *"We committed to this problem statement, and here is the evidence behind it and
  the alternatives we rejected."*

That is the **Observe → Define handoff** from [[Problem Definition]].

**Explicitly out of scope:** recruiting and scheduling research, ideation tooling,
prototyping tools, task management. Say this out loud — scope holds only when the *nots*
are named.

### How far along the process? → [[Process Coverage — How Far the App Helps]]
Deep before the prototype, thin at it, a second peak after it. Coaching concentrates at
**Define**; the record runs the whole loop and earns its keep again at **Iterate**.

### Narrow intervention, wide record #insight
Resolves the tension with wanting a repository: **active support** lives at one handoff,
but **the ledger persists across the whole project** and later stages append to it. You get
the repository without having to be good at seven stages on day one.

## Platform → **Native iPad app** #key
Decided **9 Sep 2026**. The promise is *make reasoning visible*: a claim, its evidence, the
rejected alternatives and the untested assumption form a structure, and a structure read one
card at a time is not seen at all. The payoff moment is a **team** reading its own argument
together — four people gather around an iPad, nobody gathers around a phone. The core
interaction is two panes, and comparison requires simultaneity.

Costs, named: field capture is more awkward, the nudge-at-the-moment mechanic largely goes, and
the *why-not-a-website* argument gets **harder** — it now rests on Pencil, touch, on-device
privacy and offline. Full reasoning: [[Platform Decision — iPad App]].

## How far to *build* it — staging #key
Not to be confused with how far along the DT process it helps — that's
[[Process Coverage — How Far the App Helps]]. This is about build maturity.

Purpose decides. For a course project the deliverable is **validated learning plus a working
prototype**, not a business.

| Stage | What | Time | Proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Paper playbook, 3 teams | this week | Does the intervention change what they *decide*? |
| **1** | **Wizard of Oz** — a human plays the AI nudges | 2–3 weeks | The interaction, with **no AI built** |
| **2** | Working prototype, one moment only, one team | weeks | It works unattended |
| **3** | Pilot in one real course, one semester | a term | Outcome difference vs. a control group |
| **GTM** | — | later | only after stage 3 |

**Recommendation: go to stage 2, aim at stage 3. Do not chase go-to-market.**
Your differentiator is a **learning outcome**, and the only proof of a learning outcome is a
cohort comparison. Without stage 3 you have a UI, and UIs in this space are commoditised.

> [!important] Stage 1 is the one people skip. You can test every nudge in
> [[AI Nudging — Interaction Design]] with a human pretending to be the AI. If the nudges
> don't change decisions when a *person* delivers them, no model will save them.
