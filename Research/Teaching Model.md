# 🎓 Teaching Model — Prompt or Teach?

**10 Sep 2026.** Resolves [[Backlog]] #10, which was blocking the feature list.

## Decision #key
> **Prompting in context is the mechanism. Teaching happens by naming the move *after* they
> have made it. Explicit method content exists, but it is for the instructor — never pushed
> at students.**

## Why not teach up front
Novices do not read the method before they need it. IDEO's Design Kit and the d.school
resources already exist, are free, are excellent — and go unread. That is precisely the gap
identified in [[Competitive Landscape]]: **they teach but do not accompany.** Building more
content to sit unread would reproduce the failure rather than fix it.

There is also the awareness problem: a student who does not yet know their insight is weak has
no reason to read a chapter about strengthening insights. → [[Why Students Struggle — Causal Factors]] #5

## Why not prompt only
If the app only ever asks questions and never names what it is doing, nothing transfers. The
student gets better *inside the app* and no better outside it — which fails the whole purpose,
since the goal is judgement they carry into the next project.

## The resolution — experience first, label second #key
```
1. The app asks the question in the moment          ← the work happens
2. Afterwards it names the move they just made      ← the label lands
   "That's called checking the warrant — the link between
    what you saw and what you concluded."
3. Next time, it asks without naming
4. Eventually it does not ask at all
```

Naming a move **after** someone has felt the need for it makes the label stick. Naming it
before gives them a term with nothing attached. This is also why the method reference exists
but is never pushed: by step 2 the student has a reason to look it up. #insight

## How this maps onto fading

| Level | Prompt | Naming |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** | asks outright | names the move afterwards |
| **L2** | asks outright | silent |
| **L3** | *"a question is missing here"* | silent |
| **L4** | nothing — records only | silent |

## What the instructor gets
The full method, explicitly: the Ladder of Inference, warrant-checking, the provenance split.
They need it to teach and to grade, and they will read it — they have the motivation students
lack. **The instructor is where the explicit teaching content lives.**
→ [[Scope, Platform & Users]]

## Consequence for claims
We can honestly say the app **builds transferable judgement**, because the moves are named and
the support is withdrawn. We should not claim it **teaches Design Thinking** — it teaches one
method inside it, and courses already handle the rest. → [[Process Coverage — How Far the App Helps]]
